### Phase 1: Force Control & Planar Compliance

**Stiffness $K_{trans}$ and Damping $D_{trans}$:**

$$K_{trans} = \text{diag}(K_x, K_y, K_z) = \text{diag}(0, 0, 400) \text{ N/m}$$
$$D_{trans} = \text{diag}(D_x, D_y, D_z) = \text{diag}(10, 10, 70) \text{ N·s/m}$$

- **X/Y (hand-guiding):** $K_x = K_y = 0$ with moderate damping $D_x = D_y = 10$ — the operator drags the probe freely; only viscous friction resists motion.
- **Z (constant force):** An outer-loop admittance adjusts the desired Z position to maintain $F_{z,des} = 5$ N:

$$\dot{z}_{des} = \text{clamp}\left(\gamma \cdot (F_{z,des} - F_{z,meas}),\, -v_{z,max},\, v_{z,max}\right)$$

where $\gamma = 2 \times 10^{-4}$ m/s/N is the admittance gain. The inner loop tracks this reference with $K_z = 400$ N/m and $D_z = 70$ N·s/m. Gravity is compensated via Coriolis feedforward ($\tau_{coriolis}$).

**Force monitoring:** A `Float64MultiArray` is published at 20 Hz on topic `ultrasound_scanning_impedance/status` with 23 fields including filtered force, force error, admittance displacement, per-axis position/orientation errors, commanded wrench, and saturation flags.

### Phase 2: Selective Rotational Compliance

**Rotational stiffness/damping (applied in the flange frame):**

$$K_{rot}^{flange} = \text{diag}(K_{rx}, K_{ry}, K_{rz}) = \text{diag}(0, 30, 30) \text{ Nm/rad}$$
$$D_{rot}^{flange} = \text{diag}(D_{rx}, D_{ry}, D_{rz}) = \text{diag}(1, 6, 6) \text{ Nm·s/rad}$$

- **Roll (flange X):** $K_{rx} = 0$ — free rotation so the probe adapts to body curvature
- **Pitch (flange Y):** $K_{ry} = 30$ — locked to initial orientation at activation
- **Yaw (flange Z):** $K_{rz} = 30$ — locked to prevent spinning during drag

The orientation error is computed in the world frame, transformed into the **flange frame** ($e_{flange} = R^T e_{world}$), scaled by the diagonal gains, and rotated back ($\tau_{world} = R \cdot \tau_{flange}$). This ensures the free-roll axis travels with the end-effector regardless of the probe's orientation in space.

### Control Law

$$\tau = J^T \begin{pmatrix} F_{trans} \\ \tau_{rot} \end{pmatrix} + \tau_{coriolis} + N \cdot \tau_{nullspace}$$

where $N = I - J^T (J J^T + \lambda I)^{-1} J$ is the nullspace projector and $\tau_{nullspace}$ stabilizes the joint configuration.
